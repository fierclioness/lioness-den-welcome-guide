👑 Royal Ranks & rewards

Earn experience through genuine conversation and celebrate your progress through the Pride.

Check your progress: In Discord, use Lurkr’s /rank command to see your level, XP and personal rank card.

Royal rewards: Level 5 — Den Regular; Level 10 — Pride Rising; Level 25 — Pride Elite; Level 50 — Pride Royalty.

Celebrations: The Den celebrates Levels 5, 10, 25 and 50. Your highest eligible royal rank is shown because reward stacking is off.

How to earn XP: Join real conversations in #general, #squad-up, #clips-and-highlights or #introductions. Earn 15–40 XP per eligible message, with a 1-minute cooldown per member. Slash commands and threads do not earn XP. No spam or pressure to post!

The leaderboard is members-only. Rank-card backgrounds are personal customizations, so each member may see different artwork.
