Privacy Policy

Your squad. Your vibe. Your privacy matters.

DRAFT FOR REVIEW — Not yet approved or published. Last drafted November 21, 2026.

This notice describes how FiercLioness and The Lioness Den (together, “we” or “our community”) handle information through the official FiercLioness website and the Lioness Den tools linked here, including the Welcome Guide, manual Squad Finder, and Den Missions. It does not control third-party websites or Discord itself.

1. Information you choose to share

Visitors can browse the public Welcome Guide and manual Squad Finder without signing in. If you use the Welcome Guide's optional “How did you find The Lioness Den?” question, we receive the selected referral category. You do not have to provide a name or Discord username to answer it.

If you submit information directly to our Discord channels, use support tickets, or communicate with administrators, that information is handled through Discord and is visible to the relevant participants according to channel and role permissions.

2. Optional Welcome Guide discovery question

The survey records the selected answer, submission date, and a pseudonymous day-specific code to discourage duplicate submissions. Our survey endpoint briefly processes the visitor's network IP address to calculate that code using a keyed one-way hashing method; the survey response table does not store the raw IP address. The code is a technical identifier rather than a public profile. Your browser also stores a simple “already answered” preference locally, so the form does not ask you again in the same browser.

3. Manual Squad Finder

The manual Squad Finder uses your selections, such as game, platform, preferred playing time, time zone and optional note, to build a Discord-ready message locally in your browser. The current manual tool does not automatically post or save those choices to our database. If you copy the message and share it on Discord, the information in your message is handled through Discord under that platform's settings and rules.

4. Den Missions and Discord sign-in

Den Missions uses Discord sign-in through Supabase authentication. Sign-in may involve your Discord account identifier and basic profile information made available by Discord, such as a display name. The tool stores information needed to provide and verify your experience, including weekly mission selections, completion reports, verification outcomes, achievement records, and the dates associated with those activities. Authorized administrators may review reports and supporting evidence, and administrative decisions can be recorded in a review log. Some activities may be checked using eligible Discord participation, and a verified achievement can be used to assign a Discord role.

Den Missions uses an authentication session in the browser so that you can remain signed in. Signing out ends the local session. Deleting mission selections or reports is not the same as deleting your Discord account or all administrative verification history.

5. How information is used

We use information to operate the community features you choose, recognize eligible mission achievements, support members, address abuse or technical issues, maintain security, and understand which public discovery channels help people find the community. We do not use the optional survey as an individual member leaderboard.

6. Service providers and third-party platforms

Our websites and tools use GitHub Pages or Google Sites for hosting and Supabase for selected authentication, database and server-side functions. Discord provides community channels, sign-in and social features. Links may take you to Twitch, YouTube, Kick, GRIVIO, or other websites. Those companies may process information independently under their own privacy policies. Ordinary hosting, network, and security logs may be processed by service providers in the course of operating these services.

7. Security and retention

We use access controls and other technical precautions appropriate to the features. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Our database currently contains mission selections, completion reports, verification results, achievements and related review records. Weekly mission rollover does not automatically erase old records. At the time of this draft, we have not verified an automatic database-retention or purge schedule for those records or survey responses. Specific retention periods, backup handling and privacy-request procedures must be established and verified before publication.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can skip the optional discovery question, avoid including sensitive information in a squad request, or choose not to sign in to Den Missions. You may also manage permissions and privacy settings directly in Discord and other linked services. For questions or requests concerning information controlled by FiercLioness or The Lioness Den, you may email fiercelioness43@gmail.com or contact the moderation team through the private #support channel and use the existing ticket flow if you remain a server member. You do not need to be a Discord member to send an email. Please do not post personal details publicly or provide passwords, one-time codes, or authentication tokens. The team may need a limited verification step before disclosing or changing account-linked records.

Because the discovery-survey response does not include a member account identifier and uses only a day-specific pseudonymous code, we may not be able to locate a particular survey answer from a username. Requests concerning Discord messages or accounts may also need to be handled through Discord rather than through our website tools.

9. Adults-only community

The Lioness Den is intended for adults ages 18 and older. The public pages are not designed as services for children. The community's age restriction is part of its rules and does not mean we independently verify every visitor's age.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this notice when our features or data practices change. The published version will include an effective date and should remain accessible from the official website and relevant tools.

Before publication: Confirm a reliable privacy contact, specific record-retention periods, applicable service-provider logging and any additional data practices of the Google Sites homepage or production Den Missions integrations. This review copy must not be represented as a final legal notice.
